Letter From Parents

In Their Own Words

Yichen Xu is a highly curious, future-oriented, and self-driven student with strong potential in mathematics, computational thinking, scientific inquiry, and interdisciplinary creativity. Since joining Singapore American School in Grade 3 in 2022, he has consistently demonstrated advanced analytical ability, intellectual independence, and a strong passion for exploring real-world challenges through both STEM and creative expression.

Academically, Yichen has shown exceptional strength in mathematics and problem solving from an early age. He was selected into advanced mathematics pathways at school, placing within the top 1–2% of students, while also pursuing a self-paced enrichment track through artofproblemsolving.com, further strengthening his mathematical reasoning, abstract thinking, and independent learning skills. His standardized testing results further reflect his strong quantitative aptitude, including CogAT Quantitative and Nonverbal scores at the 99th percentile and MAP Mathematics achievement at the 99th percentile.

Within school, Yichen has actively participated in coding and robotics programmes, where he demonstrated strong computational thinking, creativity, and problem-solving ability. In his Coding 6 programme at Singapore American School, he achieved distinction-level performance across creativity, communication, critical thinking, and technology application. His coding teacher, Kal Cho, described him as having "exceptional computational thinking skills" and "emerging as a strong and resourceful programmer," highlighting his ability to independently design interactive applications while demonstrating "advanced programming and design skills using JavaScript." His teacher further noted Yichen's confidence in tackling increasingly complex programming challenges and his strong grasp of computer science concepts through innovative problem-solving.

Beyond school, Yichen actively pursues computational and logical problem solving through programming and competitive mathematics. He has completed USACO Bronze and Silver-level algorithmic training, covering concepts such as graph traversal, binary search, recursion, greedy algorithms, DFS/BFS, and problem modelling. Building on this foundation, he has recently begun Gold-level training in C++, further developing his algorithmic thinking and competitive programming skills. He also completed programming courses including Carnegie Mellon School of Computer Science's Exploring Programming with Python and Kehan Python. His participation in cybersecurity and robotics activities such as BLAHAJCTF and VEX IQ reflects a growing interest in applied technology, engineering, and systems thinking.

In mathematics competitions, Yichen has demonstrated sustained excellence over multiple years. He achieved Gold Awards in the Singapore and Asian Schools Math Olympiad (SASMO) for three consecutive years, earning top school and country rankings, including Country Rank #7 and School Rank #1. His AMC 8 score of 20 (Top 10%) further highlights his strong mathematical reasoning and problem-solving ability.

What distinguishes Yichen is his ability to connect STEM thinking with creativity, communication, and social awareness. His science fiction story, "No Nation Left Behind," was selected for publication in Singapore Stories from 60 Young Minds, a commemorative collection published in honour of Singapore's 60th year of independence. Set in a climate-challenged future world, the story explores sustainability, disaster resilience, renewable technology, and global cooperation through the lens of speculative science fiction. The work demonstrates not only imagination, but also systems thinking and an early awareness of environmental and humanitarian challenges facing the future world.

Outside the classroom, Yichen demonstrates the same commitment to discipline, resilience, and long-term growth through sports. He has trained competitively in tennis since the age of seven, dedicating approximately 10 hours per week to professional coaching and development. He is currently ranked among Singapore's top junior players, achieving a World Tennis Number (WTN) Singles rating of 34.3 and a Top 40 national ranking in the U12 Boys category. Yichen was also selected for the Singapore American School Varsity Tennis Team and contributed to the team's success in winning 1st Place in the Athletic Conference of Singapore International Schools (ACSIS) 2025–2026 U12 Boys Division.

In addition, he trains regularly in swimming and achieved Singapore Aquatics Level 2 certification in 100m Freestyle and Level 1 certification in 50m Freestyle. Beyond sports, Yichen pursues piano performance and achieved ABRSM Grade 3, 4, and 5 distinctions, while also participating in Spartan endurance competitions that reflect resilience and determination.

In service and community engagement, Yichen actively contributes to sustainability and social impact initiatives. As a Student Ambassador for Greenerplus.org, a youth-led environmental movement, he has participated in coastal clean-up activities and the online Plastics-Free Challenge, promoting environmental awareness and responsible consumption among young people.

Combining his passion for technology with a desire to create positive social impact, Yichen designed and developed Plastic+ Detective, an educational online game that teaches children and youth how to identify different types of plastics and make environmentally responsible choices through interactive gameplay. Developed using coding and gamification principles, the project demonstrates his ability to apply computational thinking, creativity, and problem-solving skills to address real-world sustainability challenges. (github.com/ryannjeed-ux)

Yichen embodies many of the qualities we believe are essential for future leaders and innovators: intellectual curiosity, resilience, interdisciplinary learning, and the courage to explore complex global issues through science, technology, and creativity. He is not only a capable STEM learner, but also a thoughtful young individual who seeks to apply knowledge meaningfully to improve the world around him.

More than any single achievement, what stands out is his genuine love of learning, willingness to embrace challenges, and commitment to continuous growth. We believe he will thrive in a rigorous learning environment and contribute positively to the school community through his curiosity, character, and collaborative spirit.

Tracy & Howard — Parents of Xu Yichen · Jun 01, 2026
